# Supplementary LLM-as-a-Judge Evaluation (M5 Baseline)

## Protocol

This supplementary assessment uses `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` (temperature 0) to judge the **saved** baseline itineraries generated by `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`; no itineraries were regenerated. Each judge input included the structured user request, itinerary IDs, matched dataset evidence (activity interest groups, hotel and restaurant budget levels, coordinates), and the previously prepared four-criterion judge rubric and instructions. The four 1–5 criteria are personalization, budget alignment, itinerary coherence, and itinerary feasibility, with equal weighting. The model returned structured JSON scores and explanations, validated programmatically. Two generation failures were not scored. API 500/503/504 errors were retried; all 58 generated itineraries eventually received judge results.

## Results

| Measure | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total baseline records | 60 |
| Generated itineraries judged | 58 |
| Generation failures (unjudged) | 2 |
| Remaining judge errors | 0 |
| Overall mean (1–5; n=58) | 4.522 |
| Personalization mean | 4.103 |
| Budget alignment mean | 5.000 |
| Itinerary coherence mean | 4.466 |
| Itinerary feasibility mean | 4.517 |

These scores are **supplementary model judgments**, not substitutes for the five deterministic primary metrics (APM, RBM, HBM, IFR, RTC). The 58-score mean excludes the two baseline generation failures, unlike the primary 60-run metrics.

## Findings and limitations

The judge identified repeated activities in G05, G08, and G15 and noted incomplete coverage of requested interest groups in some mixed-interest cases. However, its scoring was not fully consistent: G05 and G15 received a feasibility score of 5 despite repeated activities, while G08 was penalized for the same type of rule violation. Some explanations treated structural compliance as sufficient evidence of feasibility; others inferred travel concerns from geographic spread without verified travel-time data. Thus the feasibility mean does **not** establish real-world travel feasibility, and is not interchangeable with the primary rule-based IFR (structural validity). No verified travel-time or opening-hours data were used to certify feasibility. The judge may also apply qualitative notions of interest diversity differently from the operational APM metric. The judge was an LLM, not an independent human annotator, and its repeatability was not separately validated.
